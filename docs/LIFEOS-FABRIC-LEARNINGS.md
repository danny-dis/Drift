# LifeOS + Fabric Learnings for DRIFT

DRIFT should absorb the strongest LifeOS Harvest and Research ideas as a discovery/change-introduction pipeline.

## Discovery pipeline

`CAPTURE → INGEST → UNDERSTAND → MAP TO ECOSYSTEM → CHECK NOVELTY → FIND CONTRADICTIONS → IDENTIFY BENEFITING PROJECTS → RANK → PROPOSE → APPROVE → UPDATE`

A proposal is not an adopted change. Human approval and/or the target project's governance remain authoritative.

## Ecosystem mapping

For every useful discovery, DRIFT should identify affected projects such as DANNY, ATHENA, NOESIS, DMR-X, ARGUS, GHOST FACTORY, GLUE, MOTE, TABBY and CHIMERA, and explain why the discovery matters.

## Current → Ideal State

Ideas should be expressible as current state, desired/ideal state, gap and candidate interventions. This turns vague notes into actionable engineering or research proposals.

## Research depth

DRIFT should scale research effort according to consequence and uncertainty: quick lookup for trivial ideas, multi-source investigation for interesting ideas, and deep/adversarial verification for architecture or ecosystem decisions.

## Wisdom extraction

Ingested sources should yield claims, insights, contradictions, relationships, novel information and implementation implications rather than only a generic summary. Contrarian or uncomfortable findings must be preserved.

## Adoption ledger

DRIFT should track discovered, proposed, accepted, rejected, implemented and superseded ideas separately, with provenance and evidence. This prevents research from silently becoming architecture.
