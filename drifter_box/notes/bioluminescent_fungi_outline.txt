# Bioluminescent Fungi Research Outline

## 1. Species Catalog
- **Mycena chlorophos** – found in tropical and subtropical forests; small caps with greenish glow; contains luciferin-like compounds.
- **Panellus stipticus** – common in temperate regions on decaying hardwood; produces a faint yellow light; emits light via a luciferin‑luciferase system.
- **Omphalotus olearius** – bright orange‑gilled fungus on oak and other hardwoods; emits a strong greenish glow; known for toxic oleaginous compounds.
- **Other notable species** – *Armillaria mellea* (honey fungus) shows faint bioluminescence in mycelium; *Foxia* spp. (recently described) exhibit continuous light emission.
