# Bioluminescent Fungi Research Outline

## 1. Species Catalog
- 1.1. *Mycena chlorophos*
- 1.2. *Omphalotus olearius*
- 1.3. *Panellus stipticus*
- 1.4. *Armillaria mellea* (bioluminescent strains)
- 1.5. *Foxfire* species (e.g., *Phengaris*) 

## 2. Mechanisms of Bioluminescence
- 2.1. Oxidative enzyme system (luciferase/luciferin)
- 2.2. Molecular pathway: reaction of fungal luciferin with molecular oxygen catalyzed by luciferase
- 2.3. Environmental triggers: stress, circadian rhythms, and injury
- 2.4. Genetic regulation: expression of luciferin biosynthesis genes

## 3. Ecology
- 3.1. Habitat preferences: decaying wood, tropical forests, temperate regions
- 3.2. Symbiotic relationships: interaction with insects and mammals for spore dispersal
- 3.3. Role in nutrient cycling: breakdown of lignin and cellulose
- 3.4. Distribution patterns and seasonal occurrence

## 4. Applications
- 4.1. Medical research: biomarkers for gene expression and cell viability
- 4.2. Sustainable lighting: bio‑lighting materials and eco‑friendly illumination
- 4.3. Bioremediation: potential for enhancing fungal degradation of pollutants
- 4.4. Inspiration for synthetic bioluminescence in biotechnology
