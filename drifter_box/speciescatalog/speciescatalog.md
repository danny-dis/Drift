# Species Catalog

## Mycena chlorophos
- **Family**: Mycenaceae  
- **Habitat**: Decaying hardwood logs in temperate forests  
- **Notes**: Known for its faint greenish glow; bioluminescent compounds include luciferin and luciferase.

## Omphalotus olearius (Jack‑o‑lantern mushroom)
- **Family**: Marasmiaceae  
- **Habitat**: Living and dead deciduous trees, especially oaks  
- **Notes**: Bright orange caps; produces a different luciferin system than Mycena chlorophos.

## Panellus stipticus
- **Family**: Mycenaceae  
- **Habitat**: Leaf litter and small logs in moist, shaded areas  
- **Notes**: Emits a weak blue‑green light; used in preliminary studies of fungal bioluminescence.
